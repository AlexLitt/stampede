# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vibe-coders deciding what to ship this week. They open Stampede to pick a hole or to type what they are building and land in a cluster. They already know the herd. They need rank, why, and a receipt, not a pitch.

## Product Purpose

Weekly chart of what the crowd is shipping versus thin empty holes that already collect money. Success: a person leaves with one cluster to skip or one hole to steal, plus a permalink they can paste.

## Brand Personality

Dry, ranked, newsprint. Voice is short and specific. Oxblood for empty money and for YOU. No SaaS warmth, no roast-for-sport.

## Anti-references

Linear/Vercel dashboards. Bento card grids. Gradient KPI heroes. CanIVibeCodeIt roast copy. MicroGaps idea-roast. Framer landing templates. Inter-on-cream marketing sites. Bar-chart races. Bounce/elastic motion.

## Design Principles

- Rank is the reading path. Number, name, why, builders. Everything else is secondary.
- Estimates stay labeled ESTIMATE. Never fake verified MRR.
- One object at a time. Hover isolates a line. Click opens a newsprint overlay: Because, Do not, Tip. Esc / outside / Close. Not a SaaS modal.
- Design serves the chart. If decoration fights the numbers, cut the decoration.
- Stampede vs Empty are two grammars. Skip vs leak. Do not mix them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body and mute. Keyboard: Esc closes the overlay; controls are real buttons. `prefers-reduced-motion` already exists; keep it. Color is not the only rank signal. Hit areas on header controls should meet 40×40.
