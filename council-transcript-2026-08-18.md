## Advisor responses

### The Contrarian (Opus)

The most dangerous assumption isn't in the drawer. It's that your builder count is a real number. Every claim downstream inherits whatever the scrape measured. If the denominator is soft, you're publishing precise-looking garbage designed to be screenshotted.

Second: empty + someone collecting money = the hole. Usually empty means dead. One person making $4k/mo is evidence it's taken, not open.

Refuse named leaders (target list, defamation, gaming). Refuse more MRR fields (ESTIMATE dies in the screenshot). Missing: method, N, date burned into the copy line and image. Last week's calls, scored. Being publicly wrong on a schedule is the trust mechanism.

### The First Principles Thinker (GPT)

The drawer must answer in ten seconds: Should I spend this weekend here? DON'T BUILD vs INVESTIGATE. Missing the causal bridge: why now, demand receipt with date/confidence, one wedge, kill condition, verdict sentence. No generic named leaders. No extra MRR. "Count me" contaminates the metric. Screenshot = self-contained decision card: verdict, trajectory, receipt, wedge, source stamp.

### The Expansionist (Gemini)

Named Cluster Kings for ego distribution. Stake cards instead of copy-a-line (dodged-a-bullet vs staked-before-the-crowd). Arbitrage pivot: exit this stampede into adjacent empty hole with crowd delta and estimated market gap. Bloomberg terminal for indie-hacker attention.

### The Outsider (GLM)

Twitter visitor doesn't know what Stampede is. Rank/builders/MRR are black boxes. Screenshot = the jokes. Need empty-because-X and time-to-first-dollar. Leaders with links: yes, to verify in two clicks. Copy line must include the row URL.

### The Executor (Kimi)

Leaders: no dataset. More MRR: no. Monday: (1) `?c=slug` deep links in copy line (2) NEW/RISING/COOLING from 8-week table (3) lane percentile. Nothing else.
